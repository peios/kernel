description: "Device-tree binding reference for TI - IVA (Imaging and Video Accelerator) subsystem."
---

The IVA contain various audio, video or imaging HW accelerator
depending of the version.

## Required properties:

```text
- compatible : Should be:
  - "ti,ivahd" for OMAP4
  - "ti,iva2.2" for OMAP3
  - "ti,iva2.1" for OMAP2430
  - "ti,iva1" for OMAP2420
- ti,hwmods: "iva"
```

## Examples:

```dts
iva {
    compatible = "ti,ivahd", "ti,iva";
    ti,hwmods = "iva";
};
```
